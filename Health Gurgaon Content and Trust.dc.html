How to find the right specialist in Gurugram when you're not sure who to see

✓
Medically reviewed by Dr. P. Sharma, MD (General Medicine)Written by the HealthGurgaon guides desk · Updated 4 Oct 2026 · Next review Apr 2027
IN THIS GUIDE
If it's an emergency (chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, heavy bleeding) don't look for a specialist. Call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.
